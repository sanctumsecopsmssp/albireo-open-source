# Contributing

**External contributions are paused** (2026-10-11) until Sanctum SecOps LLC
adopts a contributor license agreement that covers copyright and patent rights.
Pull requests from outside contributors will not be merged before then.

Contributions are accepted under the repository license and Developer Certificate of Origin 1.1 process. Add `Signed-off-by: Legal Name <email@example.com>` to each commit.

Contributors certify that they have the right to submit the work. Contributions must include tests for security-sensitive behavior; avoid secrets, customer data, CUI, and restricted third-party material; disclose known patent or license constraints; preserve relevant constant-time and zeroization properties; document interoperability changes; and pass project checks.

Protocol changes require a design proposal, threat and downgrade analysis, compatibility plan, test vectors, and two maintainer approvals including one security reviewer. Acceptance does not create a commercial relationship, certification, or endorsement.
