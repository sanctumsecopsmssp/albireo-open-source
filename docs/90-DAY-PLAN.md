# Ninety-Day Plan

## Days 1–30

Freeze the candidate commit; map public and commercial boundaries; complete provenance, patent, trademark, secret, privacy, and license reviews; produce sanitized repositories and synthetic examples; define a chain-neutral receipt and anchoring API.

## Days 31–60

Run a clean-room two-node deployment; publish deterministic vectors, conformance tests, formal artifacts, and evidence manifests; test health, readiness, shutdown, rekey, replay, downgrade, loss, and recovery; verify independence from hosted services; model test-network costs.

## Days 61–90

Launch an experimental release; recruit three managed-receipt design partners; invoice in USD without a token; measure cost per million receipts, storage, anchors, support, and gross margin; convert findings into Team, Business, Enterprise, and Regulated plans.
