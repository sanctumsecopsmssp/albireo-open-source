# Ninety-Day Plan

Revised 2026-10-11 for specification-only publication.

## Days 1–30

Freeze the candidate commit; map public and proprietary boundaries; complete
provenance, patent, trademark, secret, privacy, and license reviews; file the
ALBIREO/QZT patent claims; obtain a recorded pre-disclosure verdict for the
specification text; define a chain-neutral receipt and anchoring API.

## Days 31–60

Publish the specification under CC BY 4.0 only after the Days 1–30 gates pass.
Run a clean-room two-node deployment on the proprietary core; test health,
readiness, shutdown, rekey, replay, downgrade, loss, and recovery. Test vectors,
conformance tests, formal artifacts, and evidence manifests stay private.

## Days 61–90

Launch the managed-receipt service on the proprietary core; recruit three
managed-receipt design partners; invoice in USD without a token; measure cost
per million receipts, storage, anchors, support, and gross margin; convert
findings into Team, Business, Enterprise, and Regulated plans.
