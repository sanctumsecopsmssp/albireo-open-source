# Blockchain Commercial Model

## Product

Albireo Verified Receipts accepts authorized evidence events and returns a signed receipt, policy result, retention reference, inclusion proof, and optional public-chain anchor reference. Customers can verify receipts with the open verifier after subscription ends.

## Revenue streams

- Managed verification and policy evaluation subscriptions.
- Usage fees per million accepted receipts.
- Evidence storage, retention, legal hold, and auditor access.
- Public-chain anchor batches priced as network cost plus service margin.
- Dedicated validator or observer operations.
- Enterprise connectors, private deployments, support, and professional services.

## Safety and margin rules

Batch receipt commitments into Merkle roots. Keep raw evidence off-chain. Put no secrets, personal data, CUI, raw attestations, or reversible customer identifiers on public ledgers. Use chain-neutral receipt formats, support multiple networks, define confirmation thresholds, and disclose chain costs separately or through a clear allowance.
