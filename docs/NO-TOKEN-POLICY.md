# No-Token Launch Policy

The initial Albireo business model uses USD subscriptions and usage fees for hosted software, verification, retention, anchoring, infrastructure, and support. It does not require a native token.

No representative may announce or imply a token sale, presale, allocation, airdrop, staking program, expected listing, liquidity plan, appreciation, yield, revenue share, buyback, governance right, equity right, or investment return.

Any future crypto asset requires a separate written proposal addressing technical necessity, distribution, control, governance, consumer protection, sanctions and AML exposure, tax, accounting, custody, cybersecurity, market communications, and securities and commodities analysis. External U.S. legal and tax counsel must approve the plan before public discussion or distribution.
