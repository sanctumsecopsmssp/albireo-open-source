# Claims Boundary

Approved when accurate: “open-source protocol and reference implementation,” “designed for cryptographic agility,” “independently verifiable receipts,” “supports evidence anchoring to selected distributed ledgers,” and “formal models and reproducibility artifacts are published for review.”

Do not claim FIPS validation, certification, CNSA compliance, quantum proof, unhackability, zero-trust compliance, government approval, guaranteed finality, guaranteed compliance, or legal admissibility unless documented for the exact product, build, configuration, and scope.

A benchmark must identify version, commit, platform, configuration, sample size, method, date, and raw evidence. Blockchain immutability claims must explain source-integrity, key-compromise, off-chain-retention, and chain-reorganization limits.
