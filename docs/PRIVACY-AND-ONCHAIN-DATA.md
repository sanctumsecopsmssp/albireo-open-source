# Privacy and On-Chain Data

Collect only identifiers, outcomes, commitments, timestamps, telemetry, and evidence necessary to provide the contracted service.

Only domain-separated commitments, aggregate roots, protocol versions, transaction metadata, and other approved non-sensitive values may be placed on a public blockchain. Raw evidence and customer records remain off-chain under contractually defined retention.

Off-chain data is deleted according to contract, backup, legal-hold, and security requirements. Public-chain commitments generally cannot be deleted; users must receive clear notice before activation. Production notices and data-processing terms must identify relevant hosting, observability, support, identity, payment, and blockchain infrastructure providers.
