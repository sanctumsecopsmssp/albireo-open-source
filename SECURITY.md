# Security Policy

Report suspected vulnerabilities privately to `[SECURITY EMAIL]`. Do not open a public issue for an unpatched vulnerability. Include the affected version, configuration, reproduction steps, impact, and whether keys or customer data may be exposed. Never include production secrets or regulated information.

The project aims to acknowledge reports within three business days, triage within seven business days, and coordinate remediation and disclosure according to severity and affected deployments.

Any safe harbor applies only to good-faith research within published scope. It does not authorize privacy violations, persistence, data destruction, denial of service, social engineering, customer targeting, or access beyond what is necessary to demonstrate an issue. No bounty is promised unless a separate program expressly applies.
