# Commercial Boundary

## Open layer

The intended public layer includes the protocol, wire format, core node, CLI, SDKs, local verifier, evidence schema, conformance suite, test vectors, formal models, reproducibility tools, and synthetic community deployment examples.

## Commercial layer

Sanctum SecOps may charge for managed tenancy, enrollment, policy orchestration, verified-receipt APIs, evidence retention, blockchain anchoring, transaction reconciliation, dedicated validators, fleet operations, regulated exports, enterprise connectors, SLAs, support, training, certification services, and private deployments.

## Independence

Anyone may operate the open implementation. Access to Sanctum-hosted networks, commercial registries, official compatibility marks, support, or service commitments requires a separate agreement. The open verifier should work without a paid account.

## Revenue principle

Customers pay for operated trust, availability, retention, integrations, support, and independently verifiable receipts—not for permission to inspect or use the core protocol.
