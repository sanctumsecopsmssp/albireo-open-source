# Commercial Boundary

Decision of record: 2026-10-11, specification-only publication.

## Public layer

The ALBIREO protocol specification and wire-format description, released under
CC BY 4.0 after the related patent filing and a recorded pre-disclosure
clearance, plus synthetic deployment examples. CC BY 4.0 grants no patent or
trademark rights (Section 2(b)(2)).

## Proprietary layer

Sanctum SecOps LLC owns and does not publish the core node, CLI, SDKs, local
verifier, evidence-schema tooling, conformance suite, test vectors, formal
models, and reproducibility tools. They are available only under a separate
written agreement.

## Commercial services

Sanctum SecOps may charge for managed tenancy, enrollment, policy orchestration,
verified-receipt APIs, evidence retention, blockchain anchoring, transaction
reconciliation, dedicated validators, fleet operations, regulated exports,
enterprise connectors, SLAs, support, training, certification services, and
private deployments.

## Independence

Anyone may read the published specification. Implementing it may require a
patent license from Sanctum SecOps LLC. Access to Sanctum-hosted networks,
commercial registries, official compatibility marks, support, or service
commitments requires a separate agreement.

## Revenue principle

Customers pay for operated trust, availability, retention, integrations,
support, proprietary software, and independently verifiable receipts.
