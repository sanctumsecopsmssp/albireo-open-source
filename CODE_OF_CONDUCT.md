# Code of Conduct

Project participants must communicate professionally, respect differing experience and viewpoints, avoid harassment and discrimination, protect private and security-sensitive information, and focus criticism on technical work rather than people.

Maintainers may edit or remove abusive, unsafe, irrelevant, or privacy-violating content and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation. Report conduct concerns privately to `[CONDUCT EMAIL]`. Reports will be handled as confidentially as reasonably possible.
